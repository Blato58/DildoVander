# Vapour Chrome

The owner's October 2026 brief replaces the prior application visual identity.
The canonical specification and implementation are
[kufr/design-system/README.md](kufr/design-system/README.md) and its token/component
sources. Keep detailed guidance there rather than copying it into instructions.

Use Vapour Mist `#EEF1F5`, Chrome Midnight `#1B2430`, Steel Grey `#8A94A6` and
Digital Periwinkle `#B8C0FF` through semantic tokens. Inter serves UI and headings;
IBM Plex Mono serves data. Use calm surfaces, useful whitespace, clear active/focus
states, accessible contrast and responsive task-oriented navigation. Preserve
physical materials and content artwork. The `aw-*` namespace and
`arcane-workshop.css` filename remain compatibility APIs.

Operating tools prioritize state, control and recovery; the CAD viewer prioritizes
the model; public pages prioritize the visitor's keepsake and story. Shared visual
principles do not require identical density. Theme preferences belong to each
consumer; public private-data rules take precedence over persistent preferences.

Use installed Impeccable for visual quality, Product Design for flow inspection,
Frontend Quality for implementation and Browser Validation for rendered checks.
The explicit owner brief settles visual direction and authorizes ordinary UX
decisions without further design approval rounds. Check actual narrow and wide
layouts, keyboard, loading/error states and real generated outputs.
